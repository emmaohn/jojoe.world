Summary

Suyu and Wubi live two very different lives: Suyu is very poor living with his single father after his mother left them to marry rich, and Wubi has had everything he's wanted since he was a child... besides the suspicious death of his mother and a new stepmother he resents.

The two boys' lives are inextricably intertwined before they even meet. Will girls, family troubles, or the mysterious death of a mother be enough to tear them apart?

This series was adapted from the novel "Are You Addicted?" by Chai Ji Dan.

Review

I haven't read the original source material of the show, but I did watch the original adaptation and I DO have a few things to say!! This adaptation was a lot cuter, and a lot more normal than the first! In both shows the main characters have really good chemistry, but in Stay With Me, you can tell they like each other from the very start! Though Suyu is still the guarded and focused character Louyin is in the first adaptation, you can tell he's more open and friendly in the second. It is a lot more realistic for the two of them to become friends than in the first. Plus, a lot of the teasing from Wubi actually feel like it has a purpose and draws them closer than in the first adaptation where it feels like Gu hai is just doing things to piss off Luoyin ㅠㅠㅠ. PAR EXAMPLE, in the first adaptation, Gu hai cuts holes in the back of Luoyin's jacket so that he can offer to sew them up for him, but also because he wants to make fun of Luoyin for sewing up a hole in the white jacket with black thread, so his idea is that he will make more holes to sew them up wwith black threa dso he can call him a cow ㅠㅠㅠㅠ. Lmao, BUT in the second adaptation, Suyu's dad mentions to Wubi that he feels bad about not being able to buy Suyu a new uniform like all the other kids at school have while working a part time job he's not telling Suyu about to help move Wubi into his new house even though he has back problems and so knowing that Suyu will not just accept the uniform from Wubi, Wubi spills chili oil on his uniform so that he will accept a new one. This teasing has a lot more layers to it, and also says something about Wubi, Suyu, and Suyu's dad. There is a lot more development of the other characters in this show than just the main two, while still having plenty of time to have scenes with just the two of them. I LOVED THAT!! Ooh girl, the scene where the power goes out and Wubi kisses Suyu in class?? I dont even know how to express how I feel about that scene!!

BUT!! It's not all sunshine and rainbows! Wtf was that ending??????????? I thought the parallel was really good of Wubi being willing to die to protect the people he loves just like his mom when she was in HER car crash, but HELLO?? Why was the last scene silent?
